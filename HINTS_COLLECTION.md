# Wasgij hints collection — links only

Official Wasgij images and hints remain on the publisher's website; this repository does not store copies. Links are to verified 1000-piece product pages. **Hint 1 and Hint 2** buttons appear on these pages, but direct image URLs have not yet been independently extracted or verified. Original Nos. 01–08 use official **Retro Original 1000-piece reissues** because the archive's early Original editions are listed at 500 pieces. This does not change the owned puzzle's series or edition.
